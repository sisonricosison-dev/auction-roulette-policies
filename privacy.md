## 2. Information We May Collect or Process

Depending on the Bot's enabled features and configuration, it may process or store the following information:

- **Discord user ID:** Used to identify members in the Bot's database.
- **Discord display name or username:** Used to identify members in Bot records and auction displays.
- **Server membership information:** Used to maintain the member roster and determine whether a member is active.
- **Role and eligibility information:** Used to determine whether a member is eligible to participate in an auction.
- **Auction information:** Such as participation, selections, results, and event history, where those features are enabled.
- **Activity information:** Such as message or voice activity, only if the relevant tracking features are enabled and implemented.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the Bot may be used to:

- Maintain the server's member roster.
- Determine auction eligibility based on configured Discord roles.
- Run and manage auction events.
- Record auction outcomes and related history.
- Support authorized administrative and officer functions.
- Diagnose errors and maintain the Bot.

The Bot's use of information is limited to operating and supporting its intended features.

The Bot is intended to store its application data in a local SQLite database controlled by its operator.

We do not intend to sell personal information or share the Bot's database with third parties for their independent use.

However, the Bot operates through Discord. Discord processes information under its own policies, and information sent in Discord messages or interactions is subject to Discord's systems and policies.
